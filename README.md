# Activity 2 
## *From Information to Visual Communication*

This activity is a two-page infographic presentation. The first page explores the impact of Chemical Engineering on society and its importance in improving everyday life. It highlights how chemical engineers apply science, technology, and engineering principles to address real-world challenges and contribute to different sectors of society.

The second part of the project presents an estimated student distribution of BS Chemical Engineering students compared to different courses at Ateneo de Davao University. The infographic uses a pie chart and key-metrics panel to visualize the estimated proportions.
